# SPAM ON A TRAIN — ALL PEOPLE ARE SPAM

A crossover of Bees on a Train and SPAM: The Art of Being Consumed. Hand-off for one world-model session.

## Director

One session, one initial seed. At each closed door, stop walking, open the door, send only the next carriage cue while the doorway occludes the new set, then resume walking when the doorway is clear. Trigger individual object events separately only when needed. Observe the original can silhouette, label, gait, doorway and floor. Skip optional actions if they destabilize continuity. Do not treat these prose cues as official API parameters.

Not executed against a paid model.

## Seed — send once

Seed image: `train/seeds/spam_0_seed.jpg`

```
Continuous third-person game take, behind one living blue-and-yellow SPAM Classic can with small articulated metal arms and legs, a rounded rectangular tin body, a gold pull-tab lid and two small eyes. Every person in this world is an anthropomorphic can of SPAM. There are no human characters. The protagonist walks upright with a steady, short, planted-foot gait, keeping the same silhouette and label. He begins alone in an empty vintage railway carriage, with muted petroleum-teal walls, worn burgundy seats, curved timber ceiling, brass window surrounds and golden highlands passing outside. There are no other passengers and no bees yet. A closed teal sliding door stands ahead. Wide rear third-person follow camera, showing the whole can and feet. No cuts, dissolves, body morphs, sudden camera changes or HUD. Ordinary rail sound, metal footsteps. Every new carriage is revealed only beyond its physical doorway. Objects remain in their places unless they physically move. Bees are small recognizable flying insects, not sparkling particles. Keep the same train, direction of travel and protagonist throughout.
```

## 01 — CLASSIC / ONE BEE

Send as door 1 opens.

```
As the next door slides open, reveal a quiet passenger car populated by upright living SPAM cans in different flavor labels. One reads a newspaper; one guards a briefcase; a concession can pushes a trolley. A single bee flies from a cup toward the protagonist and stings his exposed pink meat when his lid lifts. He recoils, shakes his little arm, closes the lid and resumes walking. Keep the rear follow camera and the architecture unchanged.
```

Look for: he stung on the exposed meat when his lid lifts; he shakes his arm and shuts the lid.

## 02 — THE DINING CAR / TWELVE BEES

Send as door 2 opens.

```
The next doorway reveals a candlelit dining car of SPAM cans serving slices of SPAM to other SPAM cans. A can violinist plays. A waiter rolls past with a silver tray. Two diners argue over a plate; one opens its lid, revealing a soft rectangular loaf. A spoon rolls across the aisle. A dozen bees fly around the sugar bowls. The protagonist steps over the spoon, steadies the waiter and continues walking. Do not replace the protagonist's body or the train.
```

Look for: he steps over a rolling spoon and steadies the waiter.

## 03 — WAX AND GOLD / TWENTY-FOUR BEES

Send as door 3 opens.

```
Beyond the next door, postal-worker cans sort sealed letters and stacks of gold tins. A balance weighs a piece of honeycomb against a can. A worker stamps a parcel while another watches an enormous Victorian train passing outside. Bees crawl over wax seals. A clerk hands the protagonist a letter bearing his own face. He looks down briefly and keeps moving. Preserve the same camera distance and walk cycle.
```

Look for: he is handed a letter bearing his own face.

## 04 — MAILWORKS / DIGITAL SPAM

Send as door 4 opens.

```
The door opens into blue-green computer and postal machinery. Living can operators feed hundreds of unsolicited envelopes through a sorting conveyor. Paper messages travel in discrete physical trajectories, piling up around a filter. CRT monitors show the previous carriages and the protagonist walking toward the next door. Bees follow separate flight paths among the moving envelopes. One envelope sticks to his label; he peels it off and keeps walking. No floating text overlays, no full-screen transition.
```

Look for: he an envelope sticks to his label; he peels it off.

## 05 — CINEMA AND DOUBLES

Send as door 5 opens.

```
The next car is a cinema inside the train. A SPAM can with a pair of folded golden wings watches the traveler. Other cans turn hand-cranked cameras. A projector plays a film of the same protagonist in the first empty carriage. An identical blue can passes on the other side of a window, aboard another train. Bees fly through the beam of light. The protagonist turns his head toward the window, then looks forward. Camera stays behind him, with a small controlled rear-quarter sweep.
```

Look for: he turns his head to an identical can passing on another train.

## 06 — HOT HONEY / THE APIARY

Send as door 6 opens.

```
A greenhouse carriage appears through the next doorway. Flowering trees, painted hive panels, jars of honey, limestone recesses and a can beekeeper in a white protective veil. A Hot Honey SPAM can carefully divides a piece of honeycomb. Many dozens of biological bees forage among plants. The keeper offers the traveler honey. The traveler takes half and returns half to the hive. The aisle remains clear and the train continues its steady motion.
```

Look for: he takes half the honey and returns half to the hive.

## 07 — THE SLOP SEA

Send as door 7 opens.

```
The next carriage is a factory of endlessly copied SPAM cans, messages and synthetic images. Small duplicate cans emerge from a moving belt and stack up. A conveyor jams and several cans roll into the aisle. The swarm grows dense around the lights. The protagonist ducks, squeezes his soft pink loaf through a gap between two fallen crates if he opens his lid, then returns to his original can. If the model cannot preserve the separate can and meat, keep him inside the can and have him step around the obstruction instead. The camera never changes into a montage. Preserve the solid carriage walls and the same door ahead.
```

Look for: he ducks; squeezes his loaf through a gap, or steps around.

## 08 — THE SACRED HIVE

Send as door 8 opens.

```
The final door reveals a honeycomb-lined engine carriage. Amber hexagonal cells contain miniature SPAM cans, fragments of letters, tiny train models, flowers and film projectors. Bees and small mechanical pollinator drones fly in coordinated but distinct trajectories. A giant golden hive stands far outside the window. A slowly rotating engine drives the train. A small empty plate waits beside the exit. The protagonist opens his lid, offers a portion of meat to the waiting table and leaves honey for the bees. The protagonist remains recognizable and the train keeps moving. No final cut.
```

Look for: he opens his lid, offers meat to the table, leaves honey for the bees.

## Optional bee embodiment (separate experimental branch)

```
When a bee lands beside the protagonist, transfer control to that existing bee. Do not transform the SPAM can into the bee. The can stays in the carriage, continuing its own idle routine. The camera descends to the insect's scale, follows the bee behind its wings, then optionally adopts its wide, UV-inspired first-person view. Fly under seats, around tins, through an open gangway and toward the flowering tree in the greenhouse. Return to the original can by a physical flight path. This is a perceptual interpretation, not literal bee vision.
```
