make-photo · the Sealed Line

Seeds for the train that is a world

Each seed is a starting frame for a world-model take. It is composed in the playable train (the traveler, the carriage, the lens and what is passing outside are all real 3D positions), captured with its depth, and then dressed on that same geometry. The left picture is the blocking; the right is the frame to hand over.

The run below is the main use: one third-person walk that starts in an empty carriage. A new prompt is issued each time he opens a door, so the change of world hides behind the door and the gangway.
