Swim and camera controls

Drag the scene to look. On keyboards, swim with WASD or the arrow keys. Space rises and Shift sinks. J and L turn, and I and K look up and down.

On touch screens, drag the stick to swim and hold Rise or Sink to change depth. In the finder, drag to pan and aim. Optics has keyboard controls for bellows, pan and front tilt.

Escape pauses the dive or returns from the finder. Set the camera at your current depth. You can swim away while it stays deployed.
